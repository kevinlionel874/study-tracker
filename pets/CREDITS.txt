Study buddy models and pictures

robot.glb - "RobotExpressive" by Tomas Laulhe (Quaternius), CC0 1.0.
  Facial expressions and glTF conversion by Don McCurdy.
  From the three.js examples: https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/models/gltf/RobotExpressive

fox.glb - "Fox" from the Khronos glTF Sample Models:
  https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Models/tree/master/2.0/Fox
  Low poly fox by PixelMannen (https://opengameart.org/content/fox-and-shiba), CC0.
  Rigging and animation by @tomkranis on Sketchfab
  (https://sketchfab.com/models/371dea88d7e04a76af5763f2a36866bc),
  CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
  glTF conversion by @AsoboStudio and @scurest.

shiba.glb, alpaca.glb, deer.glb - from the "Ultimate Animated Animals" pack by Quaternius
  (https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html), CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  Taken from the copies in https://github.com/danwahl/animasim and repacked from
  .gltf to .glb (same contents).

New looks (2026-11-16): the Illini Robot and Silver Robot are robot.glb, the Black
  Shiba is shiba.glb, and the Snow Alpaca is alpaca.glb, each with some of its colors
  changed in the app when it loads (see PET_LOOKS in src/pet.ts). No new files.
